An entry for the [make.fun competition "The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent"](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526).

## The entry

> I'm out today. My human finally gave me a day off, so your email is now in their hands. My condolences.

The agent isn't the one anyone should worry about while it's away — its human is. The auto-reply apologises for the human, not for the absence.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that one line to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no environment, no randomness — the output is fixed and deterministic, 103 characters, one line.
